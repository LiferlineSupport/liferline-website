# Liferline Website — Agent Instructions

## Writing Style Rules (mandatory, no exceptions)

### No em dashes
NEVER use em dashes (—) in any copy, blog articles, product descriptions, or any other text on this site.
Instead: reword the sentence, split into two sentences, or use a semicolon.
- Wrong: "Cable capacitance — an electrical property — affects tone."
- Right: "Cable capacitance, an electrical property, affects tone."
- Right: "Cable capacitance affects tone; it is an electrical property of the cable."

### No "utilize"
NEVER use "utilize," "utilized," "utilization," "utilizing," or any other form.
Always use "use," "used," "usage," "using" instead.
The two words mean the same thing. Using "utilize" is considered a sign of poor writing quality.
- Wrong: "utilize a soldering iron"
- Right: "use a soldering iron"

## Blog Articles

- Byline on all articles: "Written by the LiferLine team with the help of AI research"
- Every article must have a References section with real cited sources
- Only cite URLs that have been fetched and verified — never invent sources
- Articles must not contain fabricated quotes attributed to real publications
